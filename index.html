Chronology of the Postponed Census (2020 – 2027)

Official timeline of the decennial Census delay, the recent resume decisions, and the roadmap to India's first digital census.

March 2020

The Pause: Postponement due to COVID-19

The Ministry of Home Affairs announced the postponement of the first phase of Census 2021 (Houselisting & Housing Census) and the updating of the National Population Register (NPR), which were originally scheduled to be conducted from April 1 to September 30, 2020, as a preventive measure against the spread of the COVID-19 pandemic.

View PIB Announcement
December 2022

The Delay: Freeze of Administrative Boundaries

To ensure operational readiness and accuracy in counting, the freezing date for boundaries of administrative units (districts, sub-districts, tehsils, and towns) was extended several times (up to December 31, 2022, and subsequently into 2023/2024), officially deferring the Census enumeration process.

Read Delay Details (PIB)
Recent Decision

The Resume: 2-Phase Census & Caste Survey

The Cabinet approved the formal resumption of the decennial population census operations. The operations will be conducted in two phases (Houselisting & Housing Census in Phase 1, and Population Enumeration in Phase 2) and will officially include an enumeration of caste categories.

View Resumption Release (PIB)
Future Roadmap

2027 Census of India: Digital Roadmap

This next census will be India's first digital census, with census enumerators collecting data using self-enumeration options and a dedicated mobile application. The main Population Enumeration phase is officially tracked to take place in February 2027, with revision rounds in March 2027.

Original Data Sources & References

National Population Projections (July 2020)

The official report published by the Technical Group on Population Projections, Ministry of Health and Family Welfare (MoHFW), detailing scientific projections for India and major states from 2011 to 2036.

Open PDF Report (Official MoHFW)
Office of the Registrar General

The official portal of the Census of India, providing comprehensive decennial datasets, state profiles, tables on religion, literacy, SC/ST, and primary census abstracts from 2011.

Visit Census India Portal
NSO Periodic Labour Force Survey (2023-24)

The Annual Report of the National Statistical Office (NSO) containing updated estimates on national literacy rates (overall 80.9%), employment status, and other labor force trends.

Visit MoSPI Web Portal

Comprehensive projections and research papers describing the long-term demographic shifts, fertility rates, and growth models of major religious communities in India.
